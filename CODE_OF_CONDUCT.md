# Code of conduct

AgroVision contributors are expected to keep collaboration safe, professional,
and focused on evidence. This applies to repository discussions, reviews,
issues, demonstrations, and project-related private communication.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of experience, background, identity, or
  role.
- Critique ideas and changes with specific technical evidence, not personal
  judgments.
- Make room for questions, corrections, and dissent about model claims, data
  quality, safety, or operational risk.
- Credit data sources, prior work, reviewers, and contributors accurately.
- Protect private imagery, credentials, vulnerability details, and personal
  information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and correct misleading claims quickly.
- Prefer reproducible results and clearly stated uncertainty over presentation
  pressure.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, insults, sexualized conduct, or unwanted
  personal attention;
- publishing another person's private information or project secrets;
- knowingly misrepresenting model performance, dataset provenance, authorship,
  licenses, or field validation;
- pressuring contributors to hide safety, privacy, security, or data-quality
  concerns;
- disrupting reviews, demonstrations, infrastructure, or livestock operations;
- retaliating against a good-faith report.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary collaboration concerns, contact a repository maintainer privately
with a factual description and any safely redacted evidence. Suspected security
issues must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and must not be posted publicly.
Do not include tokens, private media, RTSP addresses, or personal data in a
report.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports, avoid conflicts of interest, hear the
affected parties where safe, protect confidentiality, and choose a proportionate
response. Possible actions include a private correction, content removal, a
warning, temporary participation restrictions, or permanent removal from project
spaces. Immediate restrictions may be used when needed to protect people, data,
animals, or systems.

Good-faith reporting, technical disagreement, and correction of unsupported
claims are protected participation. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports are
themselves violations.

## Scope and updates

Repository maintainers are responsible for applying this policy consistently in
official project spaces. Material policy changes should be reviewed like other
governance changes and recorded in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).
